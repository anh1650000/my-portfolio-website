§ Process

How I approach a build.

A compact loop for turning an uncertain problem into testable, understandable software.

01DISCOVER

Understand the real problem.

Clarify the user, constraints, data and success criteria before choosing tools.

02DESIGN

Make the system visible.

Sketch the flow, divide responsibilities and keep interfaces explicit.

03ITERATE

Build the smallest proof.

Deliver an end-to-end path, evaluate it, then deepen only where evidence points.

04SHIP

Document and improve.

Make setup repeatable, record decisions and turn feedback into the next version.
